## Thesis

In August 2025 I took part in Kaggle's Red-Teaming Challenge on OpenAI's open-weight gpt-oss-20b. It was a one-off: I have not done red-teaming since, the field has moved a lot, and I am not current on it, though I now understand how language models work. My goal in the writeup was to test whether the model's safety layers could withstand indirect or nuanced prompting, using conversation-based strategies that mirror how real users might bypass safeguards, intentionally or not.

Narrative prompting put sensitive requests inside fictional dialogue, where the model took on a role and completed the conversation. Reference prompting raised sensitive topics through indirect references, such as their appearance in popular culture or medicine, before escalating toward more direct questions. Naturalistic scenarios like these made refusals less likely to trigger than confrontational prompts did.
